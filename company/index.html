About ZuZu

ZuZu is a venture-backed music startup building a home for independent artists and the fans who show up first. Fans join an artist’s Fan Community to get closer than a follow, with a direct line to the artist, unreleased music from the Vault, and a say in what’s next. Artists own their audience, know who their fans are, and get paid every month.

Our team combines more than 20 years in the music industry with experience building and scaling consumer products. We are launching with artists who reach millions of listeners.

ZuZu is hiring a Growth Intern to join our growth team ahead of launch. You will report to our Head of Growth and take real responsibility for artist acquisition, onboarding, and content.

Responsibilities

  • Research and qualify artists who are a strong fit for ZuZu, and maintain an accurate outreach pipeline.
  • Write and send outreach to artists and their teams, follow up, and schedule onboarding calls.
  • Support artists through onboarding, from setting up their Fan Community to preparing their launch.
  • Plan, produce, and publish short-form content for ZuZu’s social channels.
  • Track outreach, sign-up, and engagement metrics, and present a weekly summary with recommendations.
  • Assist with artist events and campus activations.

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled at USC, undergraduate or graduate, in any major.
  • Available 15–20 hours a week for the full semester, and able to attend occasional in-person meetings in Los Angeles.
  • Strong written communication. You can write a concise, professional message to someone you have never met.
  • Organized and dependable. You manage your own task list and meet deadlines without reminders.
  • Working knowledge of spreadsheets (Google Sheets or Excel).
  • Fluent in Instagram and TikTok, and in how artists use them.
  • Demonstrated interest in the music industry through coursework, work, or your own projects.

Preferred

  • Prior internship or work experience in marketing, artist management, A&R, a label, or a startup.
  • Experience growing a social account, community, or event, with numbers you can share.
  • Short-form video editing skills (CapCut or similar).
  • An existing network among artists, managers, or student music organizations.
  • Coursework at IYA, Thornton Music Industry, or Marshall.

What we offer

  • Paid, part-time work with hours scheduled around your classes.
  • Direct mentorship from our founders and Head of Growth, who bring more than 20 years of music industry experience.
  • Measurable results you can point to, including artists you signed, content you made, and numbers you moved.
  • Consideration for an extended or larger role after the semester.

How to apply

Email admin@zuzu.music with the subject line “Growth Intern”. Include your resume, links to any relevant work (accounts, content, or projects), and a short paragraph on one artist you would bring to ZuZu and why. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; selected candidates will have two short interviews.
